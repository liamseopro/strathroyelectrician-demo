Why Homeowners Call Us

Buying a home, or insurer asking for an inspection?

We inspect Strathroy homes panel to outlet and give you a clear, honest report on what is safe and what needs fixing.

  • Whole-Home Safety Review

    We check the service size, grounding, breaker condition, outlet polarity, and any knob-and-tube or aluminum wiring, then prioritize the findings so you know what is urgent.

  • Insurance and ESA Compliance

    Insurers often flag old panels or wiring on a Strathroy purchase. Our report gives you the documentation to satisfy the condition and the plan to fix it.

  • No Upsell, Just Findings

    You get an honest assessment, not a sales pitch. If the wiring is fine, we tell you it is fine. If it needs work, we quote it straight.
