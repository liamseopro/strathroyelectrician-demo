Why Homeowners Call Us

Renovating or living with old wiring?

From knob-and-tube in century homes to new circuits for a renovation, we wire Strathroy homes safely and to current code.

  • Knob-and-Tube and Aluminum Replacement

    Many older Strathroy homes still hide original knob-and-tube or aluminum branch wiring, both of which are fire and insurance concerns. We replace it circuit by circuit with minimal wall damage.

  • Rough-Ins for Renovations and Additions

    Finishing a basement, extending out, or upgrading a kitchen means new circuits done before the drywall goes up. We rough in to your layout so the trim-out is clean.

  • Whole-Home Safety Checks

    If you are unsure what is behind the walls, we walk the home, test the wiring, and tell you honestly what is safe, what should go, and what can wait.
