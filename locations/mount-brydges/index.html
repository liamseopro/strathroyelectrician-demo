Do you install EV chargers in Mount Brydges?

Yes. We install Level 2 chargers with a dedicated circuit for Mount Brydges homes, including the panel upgrade or subpanel if your service needs it first.

Can you upgrade an older panel in Mount Brydges?

Yes. Many Mount Brydges homes run older 100-amp or fused panels. We upgrade them to a modern 200-amp breaker panel, file the ESA permit, and schedule the inspection.

Yes. We take emergency calls across Mount Brydges and Middlesex for no-power and urgent electrical issues, with a local crew that reaches you quickly.
